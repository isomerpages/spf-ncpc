<p>More than 900 youths took part in the Criminal Behavioural Analysis Competition
(CBAC) 2026, a crime-solving and profiling competition organised by the
Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) in partnership with the National Crime Prevention
Council (NCPC) and the Singapore Police Force (SPF).</p>
<p></p>
<p>Since its launch in 2016, CBAC has engaged close to 5,000 youths by leveraging
their interest in criminal behavioural profiling and role-playing to raise
awareness of crime trends and inspire careers within the Home Team.</p>
<p></p>
<p>The competition culminated in a finale on 18 July 2026 at the Sands Expo
and Convention Centre, where Ms Sim Ann, Senior Minister of State for Foreign
Affairs and Home Affairs, and Mr Tan Puay Kern, Vice-Chairman of NCPC,
presented prizes to the winning teams.</p>
